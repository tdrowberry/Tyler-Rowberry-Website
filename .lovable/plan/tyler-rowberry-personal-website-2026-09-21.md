# Tyler Rowberry Personal Website

## Goal
Build a polished professional website for recruiting, interviews, and personal updates. The selected **Executive field journal** direction will present Tyler as a strategy leader with global perspective, while the uploaded lemur becomes a memorable, refined identity.

## Pages and content
- **Home:** Strong positioning for corporate strategy roles in technology, current Kellogg MBA status, selected measurable achievements, and clear LinkedIn, email, and résumé actions.
- **Experience:** A recruiter-friendly career narrative covering L.E.K. Consulting and selected earlier strategy, venture, and social-impact work, using only claims supported by the supplied résumé and LinkedIn export.
- **About:** Education, international experience, service in Madagascar and Zambia, languages, certifications, and personal interests that add context beyond the résumé.
- **Writing & updates:** A publication-style page for externally hosted writing and personal updates. It will link outward rather than include a private publishing system; until specific article URLs are supplied, it will use an honest introductory empty state and direct visitors to LinkedIn.
- **Contact:** LinkedIn and `tdrowberry@gmail.com` only, with concise availability and location context.

## Visual direction
- Carry forward the selected editorial layout: warm off-white paper, near-black type, amber details, strong typographic hierarchy, thin rules, and restrained motion.
- Use the uploaded lemur photograph prominently as the site identity, navigation icon, and footer signature without turning the site into an animal theme.
- Derive a small favicon from the lemur image and optimize the original image for fast display.
- Use responsive layouts that remain clear and professional on phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

## Assets and accuracy
- Make the supplied résumé available as a downloadable PDF.
- Use the résumé as the primary source for dates, titles, and quantified accomplishments; use the LinkedIn export for supporting narrative and earlier roles.
- Do not publish the phone number or either email address found in the uploaded documents.
- Avoid invented projects, article titles, dates, metrics, or employers.

## Navigation and sharing
- Add distinct, shareable pages with a consistent header and footer.
- Link directly to `https://www.linkedin.com/in/tyler-rowberry/` and the approved personal email.
- Add unique titles and descriptions for every page so links look credible in job applications and search results.

## Verification
- Check all navigation, external links, résumé download, email action, and image loading.
- Review desktop and mobile layouts for clipping, overlap, readability, and a clear recruiting-first hierarchy.
- Confirm the finished site has no broken pages or visible errors.

## Technical details
- Implement with the existing TanStack Start and Tailwind setup.
- Store the uploaded image and résumé through the project asset flow; keep the favicon as a small local browser icon.
- No database, login, or private editor is needed for the selected external-writing approach.
